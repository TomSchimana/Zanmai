# Daily, weekly and monthly notes

How getting a thought into the vault works, and why your words are left alone.

## Your words go in unchanged

The periodic notes, Daily, Weekly, Monthly, are the user's own writing space. The point of a capture companion is to lower the friction of getting a thought out of the head and into the vault, not to make the user learn a notation first. So capture takes whatever the user throws in and writes it down in the user's own words.

That matters most when the input is long and unshaped. A voice memo run through speech-to-text can arrive as several paragraphs of stream-of-consciousness. The wrong move is to chop it into a task here, an event there, a feeling below, that reshaping loses the thread and imposes a structure the user never asked for. The right move is to lay the text down as it came, intact, and only then look at what follows from it.

This is the same rule the rest of the vault runs on: imported material keeps its text exactly as it was, and only the fields around it are filled in. Capture extends that to the daily surface.

## Structure comes after, and never in the body

Once the raw input is safely in the note, capture looks at consequences, but handles them beside the text, not inside it:

- A mood you mentioned is also recorded in one of the note's own fields, so it stays readable in your text and usable later for a look back across the month. Your sentence is not rewritten into some special notation.
- An activity that matches a routine you already track becomes a link to it, and that routine's own record is updated. The routine is tracked in one place and your note simply points at it.
- People, organisations and subjects that already exist in your vault are offered as links, so the note connects itself without you filing anything.

None of this edits the words the user gave. The body is theirs.

## When a name becomes a contact

When a mention clearly names a real person or organisation you will come back to, a short contact entry is created so the link works and later mentions gather in one place. When the mention is a fragment or ambiguous, you are asked rather than having something created. A daily note naturally drops names that never come back, and creating an entry for each would fill your contacts with empty shells. The signal that a transient name deserves its own file is that it keeps coming back, recurrence across days, surfaced as a repeated hint, is what prompts promotion. An import is less hesitant, because there you deliberately chose to file something; a daily note stays light.

## The weekly and monthly look back

The three layers are all capture surfaces, the user can write straight into any of them. On top of that, the higher layers offer a review built from the one below:

- At the start of a new week, the weekly note gets a review drawn from that week's daily notes.
- At the start of a new month, the monthly note gets one drawn from that month's weekly notes.

Each review reads exactly one level down. If you switched the finer layer off in your editor, the higher note is simply a place to write with no review, and a monthly never reaches past a missing weekly to the dailies.

The review is written without asking, because it cannot lose anything: it either creates the note or adds a section, never overwrites, and never touches the notes it read from. It quotes your own wording: what kept coming up, what is still open against what is done, how the mood moved across the period, the notable events. It adds no subject you did not write about yourself. One review per period, and if one exists already no second is written.

## How the notes knit together

Links are offered rather than made for you, so the connections grow two ways. Your notes are indexed either way, so a thought is findable by its content even with no link at all. And at each session start Zanmai lists what it noticed: contacts and subjects your recent notes name as plain text without linking to them, ordered by how often they recur. A name mentioned once is a passing note; a name that keeps coming back is the signal that it belongs connected. You are offered the connection: from now on by default, and back into an older note only if you explicitly say so, since those entries are meant to stay as written. The result is that your daily notes become a record of what currently occupies you, connected to the rest as patterns emerge rather than on first mention.

## What this is not

Not a place for metrics. Food, sleep and workout numbers do not get sections invented for them in your daily note; those belong to their own subject. Not a filing system either: when something you mentioned grows into a subject of its own, that becomes a proper filing job with a plan you approve. What stays is the point of it: the fastest way to get a thought out of your head and into the vault, in your own words.
