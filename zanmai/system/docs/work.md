# Work that outlives one sitting

Some things finish in a reply. Others run for days: a document that goes through five versions, a piece of research that needs a decision from you halfway, a design that waits on your taste. Those get an object of their own, and that object owns the work.

## What was wrong before

The parts of one piece of work used to live in four different places, each with a different lifetime.

What the work actually was lived in the conversation, and the conversation ends. The finished file went to `doing/`. The working files sat in a scratch folder. And the question waiting on you lived nowhere at all, so it disappeared when you closed the session.

Nothing owned anything. You paid for that three ways: a specialist starting a new round had to be told the whole story again, a decision you took three weeks ago could not be named afterwards by you or by Zanmai, and there was no number anywhere for what a piece of work had cost.

## What it is now

One object per piece of work, on Zanmai's own side rather than in your folders. It holds:

- what the work is, and what finished looks like
- who is on it and what state it is in
- where the material is, where the working files are, where the result will land
- **what is waiting on you**, and what you have already decided, each with its date
- a log of what each specialist changed
- what it has cost so far, in tokens and minutes

Everything else stays where it belongs. The object points at the material; it never holds a second copy of it.

## Seeing it and answering it

The object is stored the way a database is: a table you can open, plus one page per entry holding the long form. The rows are ordinary CSV and the pages ordinary Markdown, so any editor, and any spreadsheet, opens them, and an editor that renders a table or a board from a folder shows it as one without anything to set up.

**You can answer directly in there.** Change a state, write into a page, add a note. That is a normal way to use it, not something to work around, and Zanmai picks up what you wrote. It also means you can work through what is waiting on you from your phone, because the same files are there.

## What it is not

- Not a second home for your material. Your notes stay in your themes.
- Not a task list you are expected to maintain. The specialists keep it; you read it and decide.
- Not a plan file. What Zanmai proposes to do still comes to you in the conversation, before it does it.

## The one database Zanmai writes

Every other database in your vault is yours and is left alone: nothing read, nothing written, not scanned. This one folder is Zanmai's own bookkeeping. The line runs by who owns the folder rather than by the file type, because a rule that stopped at the type would either lock Zanmai out of its own records or let it into yours.
